# Private chat for a small friend group — nothing here should be crawled,
# indexed, archived or used as training data. The whole app sits behind a
# session cookie anyway; this covers the login page and any public asset.
#
# Belt and braces: the same intent is also sent as an `X-Robots-Tag` header
# (nuxt.config.ts routeRules) and a <meta name="robots"> tag (app/app.vue),
# and known AI crawlers are answered with 403 at the proxy (deploy/Caddyfile).
